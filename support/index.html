Frequently asked questions

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the game deletes your progress, so please don’t uninstall it if you want to keep your dig.

My friend entered my code but I didn’t get diamonds.
You get your 50 diamonds when your friend digs 10 m. Open the game (or the Invite friends screen) afterwards and they are added automatically.

Can I play offline?
Yes. Your cat crew keeps digging while the game is closed, up to the offline limit shown in the Workshop. Invite rewards and ads need an internet connection.

How do I turn off music or sound effects?
Tap the gear icon at the top of the screen.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?
Gear icon → Privacy & ads consent. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
