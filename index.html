Privacy Policy — Don’t Dig Too Deep

Effective date: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Don’t Dig Too Deep — Idle Dig & Museum Tycoon (the “Game”) collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have. The Game is developed by Oleh Shevchenko (“we”, “us”).

If you have any questions, contact us at olejhashevchenko96@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • You can play without creating an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • The Game is free and supported by optional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your device’s advertising identifier only if you allow it (Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt and, in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the consent message).
  • The invite-a-friend feature uses Google Firebase with a random anonymous ID to count invited friends and grant rewards.
  • If the Game crashes, an anonymous crash report is sent through Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix the problem.
  • The leaderboard shows other players your nickname (or a generated name), your deepest run and the dig site. You can report or hide other players' names.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played (for example how deep runs go and which optional videos are watched). It is not used for advertising.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, collected artifacts, dig sites, skins, settings and similar game data) in a file on your device. This file is not sent to us. It is deleted when you delete the Game. It may be included in your own device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups), which are governed by Apple’s terms.

3. Information processed by third-party services

3.1 Advertising — Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform

The Game shows rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to double offline earnings). To deliver, measure and protect these ads, Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device identifiers: the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; otherwise other non-tracking identifiers provided by iOS.
  • IP address and approximate (country/city-level) location derived from it.
  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version.
  • Ad interaction data: which ads were requested, shown, watched or tapped.

Google uses this information to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and, with your consent, personalize ads. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Game asks for your consent through Google’s consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the Game.

How Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Invite-a-friend rewards — Google Firebase

If you use the invite feature (viewing your invite code, entering a friend’s code or claiming a reward), the Game uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Cloud Firestore:

  • A random anonymous user ID created by Firebase. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple ID.
  • Your invite code.
  • For each redeemed code: the inviter’s anonymous ID, the code used, the time it was redeemed, and whether the invited player reached the depth required for the reward.
  • Reward status: how many invited friends qualified and whether you already received the welcome bonus.

This information is used only to run the invite program, grant rewards and prevent abuse (for example, using the same code twice).

Firebase Privacy and Security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.3 Crash reports — Google Firebase Crashlytics

If the Game crashes, the next time you open it a crash report is sent to Firebase Crashlytics. A report contains:

  • the technical details of the crash (which code was running, the error, and the state of the app's threads);
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, free memory and disk space, orientation, app version and build;
  • a random installation ID created by Crashlytics, used to count how many players a crash affects. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier.

Crash reports do not contain your game progress or any personal information. We use them only to find and fix bugs. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Firebase Crashlytics data processing: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.4 Usage analytics — Google Firebase Analytics

To see how the Game is played and improve it, the Game sends usage events to Firebase Analytics, for example: a run ended (depth, blocks broken, best combo, dig site), an upgrade was bought, a dig site was unlocked, a daily goal was claimed, an optional video was offered, watched or skipped. Firebase Analytics also collects:

  • a random app instance ID created by Firebase (not your name, email, Apple ID or advertising identifier);
  • device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version;
  • approximate (country/city-level) location derived from your IP address, and session information (first open, session length).

Analytics data is used only for statistics about the Game. In the Game, advertising storage, ad personalization and ad user data are switched off for Analytics, so it is not used to show or personalize ads and it is not combined with ad data. Google keeps Analytics data for 14 months.

Firebase Analytics data collection: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

3.5 Leaderboard — Google Firebase

When a run beats your record, the Game saves to Cloud Firestore, under your random anonymous Firebase ID (see 3.2):

  • your nickname — the one you choose, or a name the Game generates (for example “Brave Whiskers #42”);
  • your deepest run in metres and the dig site where you reached it, and the time of the update.

Other players can see your nickname, deepest run and dig site on the leaderboard. Please don't use your real name or personal information as a nickname. Nicknames with offensive words are rejected. Any player can report a nickname or hide a player; a nickname reported by several players is replaced with a generated name. Reports store only the reporting player's anonymous ID and the time.

3.6 Time check

To prevent cheating by changing the device clock (for example to collect offline rewards early), the Game occasionally requests the current time from a Google web server (google.com). No game data or identifiers are sent; like any web request, the server receives your IP address and basic request information.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, precise location, health data, or payment information. The Game currently has no in-app purchases.

5. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent — personalized advertising and the use of your advertising identifier. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Legitimate interests — showing non-personalized ads that fund the free Game, ad measurement, fraud prevention, preventing cheating, crash reporting to fix bugs, usage analytics to improve the Game, keeping the leaderboard free of abuse, and keeping the service secure.
  • Performance of a contract — providing the invite-a-friend feature and the leaderboard.

6. Your choices

  • Tracking: decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt, or turn tracking off later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent: change your choice in the Game: Settings → Privacy options.
  • Ads in general: ads only appear when you tap a button to watch one; you never have to watch ads to play.
  • Local data: deleting the Game removes your progress from the device.
  • Invite and leaderboard data: to delete the invite-program and leaderboard data linked to your anonymous ID, email us at olejhashevchenko96@gmail.com with your invite code (shown on the invite screen) or your leaderboard nickname. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Leaderboard name: change your nickname any time on the leaderboard screen.
  • Other players: long-press a name on the leaderboard to report it or hide that player.

7. Data retention

  • Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game.
  • Invite-program data is kept while the invite program operates, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days.
  • Leaderboard entries are kept while the leaderboard operates, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Firebase Analytics data is kept for 14 months.
  • Other data collected by Google is kept according to Google’s retention policies.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under the age required by law in your country, for example 16 in some EEA countries), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International transfers

Google may process information on servers in the United States and other countries. Where required, such transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Security

We rely on the security measures of Apple, Google AdMob and Google Firebase. Access to invite-program data is restricted by security rules so that each player can only read and change their own records. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not ask who you are, we may need your invite code to find data linked to you. To exercise these rights, contact us at olejhashevchenko96@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

California residents: we do not sell personal information for money. Use of your advertising identifier for personalized ads may be considered “sharing” under California law; you can opt out by declining tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt or in iOS Settings.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy, for example when we add new features (such as in-app purchases). We will post the new version at this address and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the Game.
